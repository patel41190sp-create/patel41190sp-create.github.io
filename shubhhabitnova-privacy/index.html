Privacy Policy — Shubh HabitNova

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Overview

Shubh HabitNova is a habit, task and goal tracking app. This policy explains in simple words what the app does with your information, including how ads work in the app.

Your habit data stays on your phone

Everything you enter (habits, tasks, challenges, quit trackers, progress, profile name and settings) is saved only in the private storage of the app on your phone. We do not receive it and it is never sent to any server of ours. The app works without internet; only the ads need a connection.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Start.io (an advertising partner). To show and measure ads, Start.io may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Start.io, not by us, and we do not send your habit data to Start.io. Please read Start.io's Privacy Policy: https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/

Your choices about ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). You can also stop all ads by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

Notifications, and related Android permissions needed to deliver scheduled reminders (including after a restart and vibration): used only for the reminders you turn on. Internet and network state: used so ads can load. Advertising ID: used by the ad partner to show and measure ads. The app does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

Profiles on your phone

You can create more than one profile on the same phone. Profiles are stored locally to keep each person's habits separate. They are not online accounts.

PIN lock

If you set a PIN, it is a simple lock to keep casual viewers out of the app. It is not encryption. Anyone with full access to your unlocked phone and its files may still be able to reach the app data.

Deleting your data

You can use Settings > Delete Data to permanently delete a profile's data, or uninstall the app. Because your habit data is not sent to us, we cannot recover it. Data already held by the ad partner is handled under its own policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the app, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
